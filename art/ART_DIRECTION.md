## 1. Core identity: "Theatrical Ink"

**Approved working visual language:** *A contemporary, seductive theatre-noir visual novel with graphic, hand-inked 2D character portraits; mature anime proportions leaning toward indie-cartoon simplicity.*

The goal is approachable and attractive rather than hyperrealistic, ornamental or generic anime. Prioritize face readability and a striking silhouette at small UI sizes. The fantasy studio is luxurious but run by relatable adult performers rather than supernatural royalty.

**Inspirational visual principles** from *The Coffin of Andy and Leyley*: simplified face shapes, expressive eyes and mouths, decisive silhouettes, thick readable contours, restrained tonal shading, illustrative rather than photorealistic composition, strong emotional readability. **Do not replicate its characters, compositions, logo, distinctive portraits or copyrighted artwork.**

The Vice Archive's *own* direction: **burgundy theatre velvet, muted brass, dark ink, dusty rose, warm off-white, plum shadows**; more mature, glamorous fashion and warm dramatic lighting, with occasional intimate humour. Never turn the whole cast into black-haired/pale-skinned/green-eyed clones.

## 2. Character rendering rules (proposal)

| Component | Art direction |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette | Clear at thumbnail scale; identifiable through hairstyle, clothing cut and body shape; varied adults. |
| Proportions | Mature adult anatomy, stylized but believable; head proportional to portrait, no childlike/chibi bodies. |
| Face | Strong, relatively simplified face plane and expressive brows/mouth. Keep nose minimal but not erased. |
| Eyes | **Highest priority:** both eyes deliberate and consistent; easily readable irises/pupils and gaze. Moderate graphic-anime size, not glazed hyper-rendered eyes or mismatched eyelashes. No unintended eye colour changes between images. |
| Linework | Confident, smooth contours, selectively thick outer lines and thinner interior details; limited scratch/noise. Dark plum/near-black primarily, with occasional cream rim lighting; don't overtrace every skin fold. |
| Colour | Solid fills; 1 dominant shadow tone, optionally 1 highlight; avoid gradient-heavy painterly rendering and overblown shine. |
| Hair | Strong chunks/shapes, a few intentional strands; no dozens of filament hairs. |
| Clothing | Modern luxury with tasteful gothic/theatrical cues: sleek dresses, waistcoats, tailoring, lace or jewellery as accents. Avoid excessive armour, fantasy costumes and ornate filigree. |
| Skin | Different skin tones across cast; restrained coherent lighting; avoid locking every character into the same pale complexion. |
| Expression | Personality must read from brow/eye/mouth changes, not only from text labels. |
| Pose | 3/4 front portrait as core game-sprite default; easy to reuse; avoid dramatic flying hair and cinematic crops as baseline. |
| Finish | Clean, original, polished 2D game illustration; minimal texture and no forced watermark/signatures. |

## 4. Composition and usable asset types

- **First character reference:** one original, clearly adult (25+ for this test) clothed female performer, **unnamed**, centred front/three-quarter bust, neutral subdued background, whole face visible; no multiple portraits, frames, signatures, text or watermarks.
- Second variants after approval: alternate expression, modest outfit change, new pose. Keep eye shape, facial proportions, hair colour and identity constant.
- Game sprites: separate transparent character art from background where practical; expression swaps should not change scale/crop.
- Story/room art: editorial still illustrations rather than fully animated videos; backgrounds simplified with a coherent theatrical visual language.
- UI: theatrical restraint — cards/panels and off-white type on dark plum/charcoal, burgundy selections and rare brass accents. Do **not** recreate the UI of *The Coffin of Andy and Leyley* or the neon UI of Cities 2077.
- Test at full and half displayed size: face, eye direction and expression must remain readable.
- Export/working resolutions, layers and exact file formats to be determined by actual game prototype and memory performance; do not rigidly lock them in this visual brief.

## 5. Explicit failure patterns to avoid

1. Eyes: cross-eye, double iris, irregular pupil placement, asymmetrical eyelash clutter, unreadable gaze.
2. Generic AI anime: overly glossy eyes, fine filament hair, excessive skin gradients, sparkle effects.
3. Decorative noise: ornate gold border that overwhelms the character, fake signature, nonsensical text.
4. Unwanted duplicate character or multiple-panel composition in a single sprite.
5. Childlike bodies, overly youthful adult depiction or ambiguous age.
6. Inconsistent costume, face, eye shape or palette between assets that should be the same person.
7. Visually copying a recognizable third-party character, pose/composition or game UI.
8. Unlicensed model or source asset in commercial output.
