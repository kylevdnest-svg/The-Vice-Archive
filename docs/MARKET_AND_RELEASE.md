# Market and distribution notes

**As of:** 2026-11-06  
**Status:** Commercial hypotheses and research backlog; no validated revenue forecast.

## Business goal

SableInk wants **a repeatable catalogue of commercial games**, not just a single lucky release. The Vice Archive is a deliberately smaller commercial project, separate from ambitious hobby/creative projects. Aim for a polished, appealing complete game with limited production cost and clear audience.

## Audience and value proposition

- Initial focus: heterosexual male adult-game audience; selected appeal for other audiences.
- Product promise: **appealing adult artwork/content + satisfying unlocks**, delivered through a short management loop.
- Distinction from generic management games: **construct and match fantasies** with adult performers and a constrained set of meaningful choices.
- A clear finite campaign/ending, not another sandbox.

## Provisional commercial assumptions

- **$9.99** suggested early price point; **NOT approved/final**.
- **R0 art/development spend** desired. Steam fees, taxes and optional future purchases remain external expenses.
- Free demo: author **approved in principle**.
- Windows-first, single-player offline.
- No committed release date, revenue guarantee or conversion target.

## Release compliance to verify before publishing

- Current Steam restrictions on adult sexual content and live-generated AI sexual content.
- Store survey/disclosure for pre-generated AI art, and marketing/visibility rules.
- Model and asset licences, provenance and commercial rights.
- Payment/account onboarding, applicable fees and tax requirements.
- Visibility/marketing fit for content and audience.

Do not claim Steam acceptance before an actual review. No pre-approved explicit imagery or automated publication.
