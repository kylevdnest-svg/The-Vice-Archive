## Proposed by assistant — NOT yet scope-locked

The following were planning examples, not promises or approved production limits:

- Four adult performers; one venue with three upgradeable rooms.
- Four fantasy categories; five or six character attributes.
- A roughly 20–30 day campaign or 2–4 hours of gameplay.
- A $9.99 list price, subject to competitor review and value testing.
- TypeScript + React + Tauri as initial preferred implementation stack, subject to prototype fit.
- CSS expression changes, fades and simple motion; no elaborate animation.
- The first character should be an adult female signature performer, but **name and design remain unapproved**.
- Initial art test and one performer / one client / one room / one fantasy vertical slice.
- Rough timelines previously discussed are exploratory only; **no committed deadline**.
